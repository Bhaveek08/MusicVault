MusicVault does not currently include a project-level license.

Until the project owner selects and adds a license, the project is not offered under an open-source license. Public visibility on GitHub does not grant permission to copy, modify, redistribute, or incorporate the project into another product.

Third-party dependencies have their own licenses. Their notices are collected in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt); those notices do not license MusicVault itself.

Before accepting external contributions or distributing source/binaries broadly, the project owner should select a license and confirm that the selected terms are compatible with the dependencies and packaging model.
